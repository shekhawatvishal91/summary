## Dimension 1: The Core Thesis & Paradigm Shift
* **Plain-Language Premise:** Relationship self-sabotage is rarely an accidental failure of communication or compatibility; it is an organized behavioral cascade driven by an underlying core of self-hatred that generates pervasive hyper-vigilance, enmeshment, autonomy violation, and emotional exhaustion.
* **The "What Everyone Gets Wrong" Paradigm Shift:** Pop psychology often frames toxic relationship habits (jealousy, micromanagement, constant drama) as passionate attachment or lack of dating boundaries. In reality, these are symptoms of deep internal self-loathing: when you despise yourself, you cannot accept genuine love or trust your partner, so you systematically manufacture relationship destruction to match your internal state of unworthiness.

## Dimension 4: Diagnostic Nuances, Typologies & Edge Cases
* **Classification Typology:**
  - **Absence Distrust & Vigilance:** Inability to maintain object constancy or psychological safety when the partner is physically away. (Mechanism: Projection of internalized worthlessness: believing that as soon as you are out of sight, you are inherently replaceable.)
  - **Compulsive Enmeshment & Self-Neglect:** Abandoning real-world career, health, somatic care, and personal problems to obsessively monitor the relationship status. (Mechanism: Externalization of self-worth: using the romantic dynamic as a surrogate identity buffer.)
  - **Verbal-Behavioral Incongruence:** Declaring intense verbal love while simultaneously acting with hostility, constant criticism, and emotional drama. (Mechanism: Ambivalent attachment split: desperately needing attachment while punishing the partner for triggering vulnerability.)
  - **Autonomy Asphyxiation:** Disregarding the partner's need for solitude, requiring perpetual availability, and crying/guilting during normal boundary assertions. (Mechanism: Terror of abandonment driving controlling micromanagement.)
* **The Shadow & Failure Modes:**
  - Treating relational anxiety by micromanaging the partner instead of confronting underlying self-loathing.
  - Mistaking chronic emotional crying and dramatic confrontation for deep emotional intimacy or romantic passion.
  - Demanding 100% time availability from a partner, suffocating their autonomous nervous system and precipitating the exact abandonment feared.

## Key Conceptual Models
* **Self-Directed Loathing as Root Relational Saboteur:** The psychological phenomenon where deep-seated self-loathing acts as the prime causal engine for relationship sabotage. The individual's internalized sense of defectiveness drives compulsive jealousy, distrust, enmeshment, and drama to unconsciously orchestrate the abandonment they believe they deserve.
* **Pathological Enmeshment & Autonomous Neglect:** The dysfunctional relational pattern where an individual abandons their personal life, health, career, and boundaries to focus exclusively on their partner, while simultaneously violating the partner's need for autonomy through demands for perpetual availability.
* **Anxious Hyper-Surveillance & Distrust:** The inability to maintain emotional security and trust when a partner is physically absent or engaged in independent activities, leading to hyper-vigilance, accusations, and controlling behaviors.
* **Inverse Sarcastic Prescriptive Framework:** A therapeutic or communicative diagnostic technique that enumerates destructive behaviors in the form of direct, sarcastic prescriptive advice ('how to lose someone'), triggering psychological reactance and immediate clarity regarding self-destructive tendencies.
