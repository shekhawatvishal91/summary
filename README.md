## 🏛️ Prime Philosophy: The Dual-Product Architecture

Standard knowledge bases degrade deep treatises into superficial summaries or atomic bullet points. **KB-OS** permanently bifurcates and protects two distinct knowledge assets:

- **Product A (Source-Specific Reconstruction):** Exhaustive, chapter-faithful analytical codices satisfying the *Golden Test of Total Replacement* (the reader never needs to open the original book to extract mechanisms, models, scripts, or nuances).
- **Product B (Integrated Knowledge Tree):** An interconnected ontological graph mapping canonical concepts, empirical claims, dialectical contradictions, and master syntheses across all contributing sources.

Neither product replaces or dilutes the other.
